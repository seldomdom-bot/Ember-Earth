Our practice

The people by the fire.

Ember & Earth is a small, hands-on practice — woodfired heat and unhurried bodywork, offered with care among the gardens and bushland of Yarra Valley ECOSS.

We started Ember & Earth from a simple belief: that heat and touch belong together. A woodfired sauna opens the body; skilled hands take it the rest of the way. Set on Wurundjeri Country at ECOSS, our space is unpolished and quiet on purpose — crackling fire, cedar steam, birdsong, and time that moves a little slower.

Between us we bring decades of bodywork and a genuine love of the ritual of the sauna. Whether you come for a thirty-minute social sweat or the full sauna-and-massage journey, you're in considered hands.

Meet the practitioners

Two pairs of hands.

Myotherapist · Yumeiho Therapist

Dom McKay

Bodywork & the woodfire

With thirteen years of clinical experience, Dom blends myotherapy and Yumeiho therapy into deep, considered treatments — equally at home easing a stubborn knot or guiding a whole-body reset. A sauna and health enthusiast through and through, he's the one keeping the fire fed and the barrel at the perfect heat.

His work draws on dry needling, cupping, deep-tissue massage and assisted stretching, tailored to what your body actually needs on the day.

Lomi Lomi Massage Therapist

Leanne Hall

Flowing Hawaiian bodywork

Leanne practices lomi lomi — the traditional Hawaiian massage known for its long, flowing, rhythmic strokes that work the whole body as one. It's a deeply relaxing, almost meditative style of bodywork that pairs beautifully with time in the sauna.

Warm, intuitive and unhurried, Leanne creates the kind of treatment you sink into and lose track of time inside.
