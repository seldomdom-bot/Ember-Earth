Inside the Journey

Two ancient rituals,
ninety minutes.

Heat to open the body, then slow hands-on work to release it. Together they go further than either could alone.

First · the heat
30 minutes

The woodfired sauna

Step into the barrel and let dry woodfired heat do its quiet work — easing tight muscles, opening the lungs, melting the week away. Crackling fire, cedar scent, birdsong through the window.

Then · the touch
60 minutes

The massage

Warmed and softened, you move to the table for a full hour of remedial & relaxation massage. With the body already loose from the heat, the work lands deeper and gentler.

What to expect

A gentle rhythm of
heat & stillness.

Arrive a little early

Come 10 minutes before to settle in, change, and meet your therapist. The slowing down starts at the gate.

The heat, your way

Sit high for intensity or low for a softer warmth, and step out to cool whenever you like. Your body sets the limit.

Tailored hands-on work

Tell us where you carry tension. Each massage is shaped to you — from deep remedial release to slow, restorative pressure.

Bring almost nothing

Towels, robe, water and herbal tea are provided. Bring a swimsuit if you'd prefer one in the sauna, and an open mind.

Ease back out

Afterwards, linger in the gardens with a cup of tea. Hydrate, move slowly, and let the calm carry into your day.

Looked after

Pregnant, recovering from injury, or managing a health condition? Let us know when booking and we'll adapt the session safely.

Why woodfired

Fire is the
whole point.

Electric saunas are convenient. A woodfired barrel is something else entirely — a living heat with the soft crackle of flame, the scent of timber and a warmth that feels rounder and deeper. It asks you to slow down, the way good rituals always have.

  • Lower footprint. Sustainably sourced local firewood instead of a constant draw on the grid — fitting for a community built around regeneration.
  • A richer heat. Woodfire gives a gentle, enveloping warmth and that unmistakable scent and sound you simply can't bottle.
  • Rooted in place. Heated and held within the gardens of Yarra Valley ECOSS, among the people growing a more sustainable valley.
Find us

Tucked in the
gardens at ECOSS.

You'll find the barrel at Yarra Valley ECOSS — a thriving community environment hub and organic farm in Wesburn, home to a much-loved produce market, food gardens and a native nursery. It's about 70km east of Melbourne, deep in the Upper Yarra.
